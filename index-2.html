3 OSCILLATORS · 6 WAVEFORMS

Fully independent VCOs — each with its own octave, semitone, and wave. Choose from sawtooth, sharkfin, ultrasaw, sine, pulse, and a Buchla-style wavefolder. Per-VCO drift adds organic instability.

▽

DUAL FILTER STACK + HPF

Two lowpass filters in series — LPF 1 with optional Moog-style ladder cascade, LPF 2 for additional colour and resonance. High-pass filter at the chain head. Full filter envelope and LFO modulation.

⊡

64-STEP SEQUENCER

Four pages of 16 steps. Long-press any step to open the Step Inspector and lock its waveform or filter cutoff. A secondary 4-step polyrhythm sequencer B runs simultaneously.

◈

SATURATION + COMPRESSION

A tanh waveshaper at 4× oversampling with a slow LFO modulating drive depth. An optical compressor with musical pumping, capped by a brick-wall limiter at −3 dBFS.

↻

DIGITAL DELAY

Four dedicated controls: wow (slow tape pitch drift), flutter (fast wobble), tone (feedback brightness), and loss (progressive darkening of repeats). 10ms to 1.5s range.

∿

REVERB · MIDI · SYNC

Convolution shimmer reverb. Full MIDI support with CC mapping and external clock sync. A sync pulse output drives hardware gear on the beat. 8 built-in presets.

MUTATE ENGINE

Sequences that
change themselves.

The Mutate panel turns the sequencer into a living system. Each page runs its own direction and speed. Entropy, modal lock, interval freeze, and octave collapse reshape the melody over time — all reversible, all playable in real time.

⟳ DIRECTION · PER PAGE

Each of the four sequencer pages has its own direction — forward, reverse, pendulum (bounce), or random (shuffled order, cached until re-armed). Mix directions across pages for polyrhythmic counterpoint.

⚡ RATE · PER PAGE

Each page runs at ½×, 1×, or 2× the master clock independently. A double-speed page covers 16 steps in half the time — instant rhythmic density without touching BPM.

≈ ENTROPY

On each tick, active steps may jump to a fifth or octave of their original root note. Wave locks randomise. Filter overrides drift. The melody stays harmonically related but never stays still.

⬡ MODAL LOCK

Constrain all entropy jumps to a chosen mode. Notes that land outside get snapped to the nearest degree — entropy can run high while the melody stays tonal. OFF MAJMIN DORPHR LYDMIX LOC

〰 INTERVAL FREEZE

Replace the random jump pool with a single interval. All movement becomes that one interval up or down from each note's root. Combine with modal lock for very structured results. OFF m2M2 m3M3 P4TT P5m7 M7

↓ OCTAVE COLLAPSE

Each tick, any note more than an octave away from its root is pulled one octave closer. Turn it on after entropy has scattered the register — the melody gradually converges back toward where you put it.
