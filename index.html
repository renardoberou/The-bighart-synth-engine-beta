MUTATE ENGINE

Sequences that
change themselves.

Per-page direction and rate. Entropy, modal lock, interval freeze, and octave collapse — all live, all reversible.

⟳ DIRECTION · PER PAGE

Forward, reverse, pendulum, or random — independently per page. Mix directions for polyrhythmic counterpoint.

⚡ RATE · PER PAGE

Each page at ½×, 1×, or 2× the master clock. Instant rhythmic density without touching BPM.

≈ ENTROPY

Steps jump to fifths and octaves of their root note. Wave locks shift. Filter overrides drift. Never static.
